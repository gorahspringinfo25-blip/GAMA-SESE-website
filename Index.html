Who We Are

A Community Built on Unity & Strength

GAMA-SESE ZAGUSAVEH INC is an incorporated association of the GAMA Clan, based in Gahuku LLG, Goroka, Eastern Highlands Province. We bring together individuals, families, and businesses of our clan under a common vision — to uplift our community, preserve our cultural identity, and create lasting opportunities for all.

Rooted in the rich traditions of the Eastern Highlands, our association bridges the wisdom of our ancestors with the needs of today — ensuring the GAMA Clan remains strong, united, and prosperous for generations to come.

What We Stand For

Four Pillars of Our Work

Every initiative we undertake is guided by our four core areas of focus, ensuring we serve all dimensions of our community's needs.

🤝
Community Development & Welfare

Supporting families in need, funding community infrastructure, and ensuring the health and wellbeing of our members and their households.

💼
Business & Trade

Connecting members with trade opportunities, facilitating access to markets, business training, and financial literacy to drive economic independence.

🪶
Cultural Heritage Preservation

Celebrating and safeguarding our unique traditions, languages, and customs for future generations through events, documentation, and education.

🎓
Youth & Education Programs

Investing in the next generation through scholarships, mentorship, skills training, and leadership development tailored for young Papua New Guineans.
